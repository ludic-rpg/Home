If something breaks, if your table setup is weird, or if you have ideas for the app, there is a feedback channel on the [Ludic RPG Discord](https://discord.gg/WYQMvQcYgP). I will answer questions there.

## A review helps a lot

I did not put a "Do you like this app? rate it" popup inside the app. I hate those popups, and in this case it would also break immersion or add friction for the GM.

But if the Motion Tracker helps your table, taking the time to rate it or write a short review on the App Store or Google Play is extremely helpful.

## Please send me a photo of your table!

One thing I would really love is to collect pictures from tables using the Motion Tracker.
I will not publish anything where people can be identified without permission, but I would be very happy to print those pictures and keep them close to my desk. You can send your photo to me by DM on the [Ludic RPG Discord](https://discord.gg/WYQMvQcYgP).

## What happens next

For the Motion Tracker, my priority is simple: fix bugs if they appear, answer questions, and collect feedback from real sessions. The tablet support is meh... It works, but it's not super nice. The app was designed for phones. It's not a priority yet, but I'll see if I can improve it over time.

I am currently focused on two projects: S.I.X, a homemade TTRPG, and [Ludic Field](https://field.ludicrpg.com/). The Motion Tracker is part of a bigger Ludic RPG picture, and I have ideas for where it can go next. It will just take time, and probably a few more strange technical detours.
