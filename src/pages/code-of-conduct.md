<h2>We respect you</h2>
</div>

<div>
<strong>Creative honesty.</strong><br>
If we use someone's work, they're named and linked. If we miss a credit, tell us and we'll fix it fast.<br>
AI may be part of a project's creative process. When it is, we're upfront and open about it.
</div>

<div>
<strong>No ads. Ever.</strong><br>
We'll never put ads on our content. No popups begging for consent. We respect your privacy. We pay Fathom Analytics for anonymous visit stats. We don't preload YouTube players and use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com.
</div>

</div>
</section>

<section class="wireframe-border" id="you-respect-the-work">
<div class="content-grid">

<div>
<h2>You respect the work</h2>
</div>

<div>
<strong>Fair use works both ways.</strong><br>
If you use something from Ludic, please credit the creator named on the project and link back. We're genuinely enthusiastic about sharing our work and enabling collaboration or reuse of it.
</div>

<div>
<strong>Funding.</strong><br>
Ludic RPG is self-funded. We create and maintain the work on our own time and budget.<br>
If you choose to support Ludic, we'll always explain how the funds are used while keeping our creative freedom.
</div>
